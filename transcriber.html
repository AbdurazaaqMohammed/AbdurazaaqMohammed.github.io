Keys & Security

Locked

Keys are encrypted with your password and stored only on this device. They are only sent to the provider you select. This protects against someone reading your saved browser data, but not against malware, malicious extensions or anyone with access while unlocked (auto-locks after 15 min idle).

  • Gemini — Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in with a Google account, and click "Create API key". Free tier is enough for this app.
  • Groq — Create a free account at console.groq.com, open "API Keys" and create a new key.
  • Hugging Face — Go to huggingface.co/settings/tokens and create a new token (Read access is enough). Whisper models may be rate-limited on free accounts.
  • OpenRouter — Create a key at openrouter.ai/keys. Several ":free" models are available without credits.
